# Technology Roadmap 2026-2028

**Author:** Robert Lefebvre, Vice President, Technology · **Status:** approved by the executive committee, January 2026

This page gives the direction for the next three years. It is not a project plan: each team keeps its own plan and its own dates.

## Where we are

- 16 systems, run by 7 teams.
- One data centre in Montreal, and a small cloud footprint that started in 2024.
- Two things hold us back: the servers of the Policy Database, which reach the end of their support in March 2027, and the Legacy Policy System, which only one person knew well.

## Four priorities

### 1. Out of the data centre

By the end of 2028, nothing new is installed in the data centre. The Policy Database moves first, in 2026. The Data Warehouse and the claims systems follow in 2027 and 2028.

### 2. One way in

Every system signs in through Single Sign-On, people and machines alike. Shared passwords and shared keys disappear. Two of our systems already work this way, and the others follow.

### 3. Customers and brokers serve themselves

The Customer Portal becomes the first place to buy, change, and claim, in French and in English. The Broker Portal gets the same claims information as our own staff.

### 4. Decisions from data

Pricing, underwriting, and fraud detection use the same data, refreshed every night at first, and during the day by 2028.

## What we will not do

- No big-bang replacement of Policy Admin before 2029.
- No new system that only one person can run.

## How we follow up

The Architecture Review Board looks at this roadmap twice a year. The team leads report on their part every quarter.
