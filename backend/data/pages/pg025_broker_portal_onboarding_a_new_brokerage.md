# Broker Portal: onboarding a new brokerage

**Owner:** Catherine Leblanc (Digital Channels)

How a new brokerage gets on the Broker Portal. Five working days, if nothing is missing.

## What we need from the broker relations team

- The signed distribution agreement. We don't read it, we just need to know that it exists
- The brokerage's legal name and its licence number
- One administrator at the brokerage: name, email, phone
- The products they may sell: home, car, or both
- The provinces they may sell in

## Steps

| Day | Step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create the brokerage and its administrator | Digital Channels |
| 1 | The administrator gets an invitation by email, valid for 7 days | Automatic |
| 2 | The administrator creates the accounts of their brokers | The brokerage |
| 2 to 4 | Each broker follows the one-hour online training | The brokerage |
| 5 | We switch on quoting and issuing | Digital Channels |

## Things that go wrong

- **The invitation expires.** Half of the administrators open it after the 7 days. Send a new one from the brokerage page.
- **A broker cannot quote in Ontario.** The province was not ticked at step 1.
- **The brokerage wants its logo on the quote PDF.** Not possible today. It is on the wish list.
- **The portal goes back to English after login.** That was a bug, fixed in August 2026. If a broker still sees it, ask them to choose their language once more.

## After onboarding

Questions from brokers go to the broker support line, not to us. We only get a ticket if the portal itself is broken.

## Leaving

When a brokerage stops working with us, broker relations tells us, and we block the accounts the same day. The brokerage's clients stay with Tamberly.
