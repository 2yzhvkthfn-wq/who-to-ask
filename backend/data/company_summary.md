# Tamberly Insurance: company summary

**Tamberly Insurance is a fictional company.** It doesn't exist, and everything on this page is made up.

It is a home and car insurance company based in Montreal. It works in French and English, and it sells its policies through insurance brokers and directly to customers. Its technology department has 7 teams, and together they own 16 systems.

This page lists the teams and the systems only. It says nothing about the people, or about who worked on what.

## Claims Platform

Builds and runs the systems used to receive, handle, and pay claims.

- **Claims API:** Lets other internal systems read and update claim data.
- **Claims Workbench:** The app that claims adjusters use to review, assess, and pay claims.
- **Claims Intake:** Receives new claims from the Customer Portal, the phone line, and brokers, and opens the claim file.

## Policy Systems

Builds and runs the systems that hold and manage insurance policies.

- **Policy API:** Lets other internal systems read policy and coverage data.
- **Policy Database:** The main database of policies, coverages, and policy changes.
- **Policy Admin:** The app that staff use to issue, change, renew, and cancel policies.
- **Legacy Policy System:** The old policy system. It is read-only and being retired, and it still holds the policies issued before 2019.

## Pricing & Underwriting

Builds the systems that set prices and decide which risks the company accepts.

- **Pricing Engine:** Calculates the price (the premium) of a quote or a renewal from rating tables, such as the prices by region.
- **Underwriting Rules:** The automatic rules that accept or decline a new policy request, or send it to a person for review.

## Digital Channels

Builds the websites that customers and brokers use.

- **Customer Portal:** The website where customers see their policies, pay, and report a claim, in French and English.
- **Broker Portal:** The website where insurance brokers get quotes and manage their clients' policies.

## Data & Analytics

Runs the company's data, reports, and prediction models.

- **Fraud Scoring Model:** A model that gives each new claim a fraud-risk score.
- **Data Warehouse:** The central store of policy and claims data used for reports and analysis.

## Infrastructure & Security

Runs the cloud, the release tools, and employee sign-in, and looks after IT security.

- **Cloud Platform:** The company's cloud environment: accounts, networks, and servers.
- **Deployment Pipeline:** Builds, tests, and releases the code of every team.
- **Single Sign-On:** The employee login for internal tools, with multi-factor authentication.

## Technology Office

Technology leadership, architecture, and project coordination across all teams.

This team owns no system.

## Words to know

- **Policy:** The insurance contract between the company and a customer.
- **Coverage:** What a policy protects against, for example fire or theft.
- **Premium:** The price a customer pays for a policy.
- **Claim:** A customer's request to be paid after a loss, for example after a car accident.
- **Underwriting:** Deciding whether to insure someone, and on what terms.
- **Broker:** An independent seller who sells the company's policies to customers.
- **Claims adjuster:** The employee who reviews a claim and decides what is paid.
